## 👨‍💻 About Me

Hi there! 👋  
I’m **Felipe**, a **Senior Quality Assurance professional** with **6+ years of experience** working on **large-scale digital products**, mainly **e-commerce platforms**, collaborating closely with **Product, Engineering, and Business** teams.

I believe **quality is not a phase — it’s a process**.  
My role goes far beyond testing screens: I help teams **prevent risks, define clear acceptance criteria, analyze impacts**, and deliver **stable, scalable products** with real value for users.

---

## 🔍 My Quality Approach

- **Quality as Strategy**  
  Active participation in refinements, planning, and release decisions — especially during **high-traffic scenarios** (Black Friday and major campaigns).

- **Analytical & Risk-Based Testing**  
  Functional, regression, exploratory, smoke, sanity, and UAT testing focused on **real user journeys**.

- **E-commerce Expertise**  
  Deep validation of **checkout, payment methods, cart, coupons, promotions, and integrations** (VTEX).

- **Automation with Purpose**  
  Automation as a **support to quality strategy**, not the final goal.

- **Clear Communication**  
  Bug reports prioritized by **impact, risk, and business value**.

> A strong QA is not the one who finds the most bugs —  
> it’s the one who helps prevent issues before they reach users.
